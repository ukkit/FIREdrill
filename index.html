FIREdrill

FIRE stands for Financial Independence, Retire Early. The idea is to build a corpus large enough that its returns can pay your household's living costs for the rest of your life, so working becomes a choice rather than a necessity. The size of that corpus is your FIRE number. It depends on what you spend, how long the money has to last, inflation, and the returns you expect.

This page works it out from your own figures. Enter what your household spends each month and each year, your loan EMIs, your existing investments and monthly SIPs (yours and your spouse's), any income you expect after retiring, and big future goals. It then shows your FIRE number at your chosen retirement age, whether your savings are on track to reach it, how long your money would last if you stopped working tomorrow, and how much further it stretches if you spend less after retiring.

If you cut post-retirement spending
